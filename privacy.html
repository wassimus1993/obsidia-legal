Obsidia Privacy Policy

Effective date: 21 November 2026

In short. Obsidia has no accounts and no servers of ours that receive your data. Your playlist details and viewing history stay on your device. We don't sell your data, show ads or track you. The app connects to the sources you add, and to a few services needed for purchases, casting and voice search, described below.

About Obsidia

Obsidia ("the app", "we") is a media player for iPhone, iPad and Android. It plays streams from sources you add yourself: an Xtream Codes account, an M3U playlist, a direct stream link or a video file on your device.

Obsidia does not provide any content. We do not host, sell, link to or recommend any TV channels, films, series or playlists. You are responsible for making sure you have the right to use the sources you add.

Information that leaves your device

The app only sends data where it needs to, to do what you asked:

  • Your own provider's server. To sign in, load your lists and guide, and play streams, the app connects to the server or playlist link you added, using the details you entered. That provider sees your connection as it would with any player. Their handling of your data is covered by their own terms, not this policy.
  • Image hosts. Posters, backdrops and channel logos are loaded from the web addresses your provider supplies. Those hosts can see your device's IP address, as with any image on the web.
  • Purchases (Obsidia Premium). Payments are handled entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play); we never see your payment details. To check whether you have Premium, the app uses RevenueCat, Inc., which processes your purchase receipts, an anonymous app user identifier, your store country and basic device information (model, operating system and app version) on our behalf. This is not linked to your identity and is not used for tracking or advertising. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.
  • Voice search. When you tap the microphone, Obsidia listens until you stop speaking and turns what you said into text using your device's speech recognition. Where your device can recognise the language offline, this happens entirely on your device. Otherwise the audio is sent to Apple (on iPhone and iPad) or Google (on Android) to be transcribed, under their privacy policies; Obsidia tells you before the first time this happens for each language. Obsidia never records or stores your voice and never sends it to us. The resulting text is used only to search your own playlist and is kept only in your recent searches on this device, where you can delete it. Microphone and speech recognition access are asked for only when you first use voice search and can be turned off in Settings at any time.
  • Cast and AirPlay devices. When you choose to cast with Chromecast, the app sends the stream address and title to the device you picked on your network. AirPlay sends audio or video through Apple's system to the device you picked.
  • Crash reports and statistics from the stores. If you have agreed to share analytics with app developers in your device settings, Apple or Google may give us crash reports and anonymous, aggregated statistics (such as installs and crash counts) under their own privacy policies. If you test a beta through TestFlight, Apple shares the crash logs, device details and any feedback you choose to send with us.

Local network. The app asks for permission to use your local network so it can find Chromecast devices. It only uses that permission for casting.

What we don't do

  • We don't run accounts. There is nothing to sign up for with us.
  • We don't receive or store your playlist details, passwords, stream links, voice or viewing history on our servers.
  • We don't sell or rent your personal data.
  • We don't track you across other apps or websites, and don't use your data for advertising.
  • We don't provide, host or distribute any TV channels, films or series.

Keeping and deleting your data

Data on your device stays until you remove it. Deleting a playlist in the app removes its details, lists, guide and history from the device. Uninstalling the app removes the rest. Purchase records are kept by Apple or Google and by RevenueCat for as long as needed to provide your purchase and as required by law.

Security

Passwords are kept in the operating system's secure storage. The app only sends them to the server you entered them for. No storage or transfer is perfectly secure, but we don't hold your details on our side at all.

Children

Obsidia is not directed at children under 13, or under the minimum age required in your country. We don't knowingly collect personal data from children.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the EU or UK GDPR, or California law), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data about you. Almost everything is on your own device, where you can delete it yourself. For purchase records or anything else, contact us and we'll help.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the effective date above. If a change is significant, such as adding ads or analytics, we'll tell you in the app.
